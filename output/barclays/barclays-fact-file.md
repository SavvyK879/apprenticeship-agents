## 1. Company Overview

Barclays is a British universal bank. Its own listing states it has "over 325 years of expertise in Banking",
operates in **39 countries**, and employs around **90,000 people** "to help move, lend, invest and protect money
for customers and clients worldwide" [2].

* **Purpose:** "Working together for a better financial future." [1]
* **Divisions relevant to early careers:** Barclays UK, UK Corporate Banking, Private Bank and Wealth Management,
  Investment Banking, Global Transaction Banking, and the US Consumer Bank [4].
* **Financial position:** Barclays reported £9.1bn profit before tax for 2025, up 12% from £8.1bn, and raised its
  targets through 2028 to a return on tangible equity above 14% [8].
* **Technology posture:** Barclays publicly credits AI as a key driver of its cost reductions and efficiency gains
  [8]. In June 2026 it invested in CommonAI, a UK initiative bringing together "startups, enterprises, engineers
  and academics to develop, test and deploy AI systems at scale" [6].

**Northampton site:** the role is at 1234 Pavilion Drive, Northampton NN4 7SG [2]. Barclays describes it as an
"environmentally friendly campus... designed with people in mind. With cafés, collaboration spaces, green spaces,
wellbeing areas, parking, public transport links and a shuttle bus service" [2].

## 2. Values

Barclays runs **two** named frameworks, and the job listing says apprentices are expected to demonstrate both [2].

**The five Values — described by Barclays as "our moral compass; the fundamentals of who we are and what we believe
is right"** [1]:

| Value | Barclays' own wording [1] |
| --- | --- |
| **Respect** | "We harness the power of inclusion and opportunity in our business, trust those we work with, and value everyone's contribution" |
| **Integrity** | "We operate with honesty, courage, transparency and fairness in all we do" |
| **Service** | "We act with empathy and humility, putting the people and businesses we serve at the centre of what we do" |
| **Excellence** | "We set high standards for what we do, championing innovation and using our energy, expertise and resources to make a positive difference" |
| **Stewardship** | "We are passionate about leaving things better than we found them" |

**The Mindset — "the operating manual for how we behave"** [2]:

* **Empower**
* **Challenge**
* **Drive**

The listing states it directly: "All colleagues will be expected to demonstrate the Barclays Values of Respect,
Integrity, Service, Excellence and Stewardship... They will also be expected to demonstrate the Barclays Mindset –
to Empower, Challenge and Drive" [2].

**What Barclays says it wants from apprentices beyond grades:** "we're also looking for commitment and enthusiasm.
Our Apprenticeships place deep investment in you as an individual. That means we need you to match that commitment:
you'll turn up every day ready to work hard, push yourself, and think differently about what you're capable of" [2].

**Unverified opinion:** the Values and the Mindset are almost certainly the assessment framework for the interview
and group activity. Five values plus three mindset words is eight prompts. Preparing a story for each of Respect,
Integrity, Service, Excellence, Stewardship, and one showing Challenge, is a direct map to what gets scored.

## 4. Role Requirements

**Exact title:** 2027 Technology Developer Degree Apprenticeship Programme Northampton [2]
**Posted:** 9 September 2026. **Employment type:** Apprentice [2]
**Location:** 1234 Pavilion Drive, Northampton, Northamptonshire, NN4 7SG [2]
**Salary:** £25,200 per year plus benefits [2]. (The tracker says £25,000 [9] — the listing says £25,200.)
**Programme dates:** August 2027 to August 2031 [2]. Tracker records length as 3–4 years [9].
**Qualification:** Digital & Technology Solutions Professional Degree Apprenticeship [2]. Barclays adds a caveat:
"Due to the evolving nature of Apprenticeships, a new Apprenticeship might become available and if deemed more
relevant to your role, we'll change the Apprenticeship before you start" [2].
**Training provider:** the tracker records Manchester Metropolitan University [9]. Barclays does not name the

**What the role is:** "Technology Developers build and improve the systems that support Barclays. You'll learn
software engineering practices, collaborate with experienced teams and help create reliable, secure digital
services" [2].

**Stated purpose of the role:** "To design, develop and improve software, utilising various engineering
methodologies, that provides business, platform, and technology capabilities for our customers and colleagues" [2].

**Accountabilities, quoted from the listing** [2]:

* "Development and delivery of high-quality software solutions by using industry aligned programming languages,
  frameworks, and tools. Ensuring that code is scalable, maintainable, and optimized for performance."
* "Cross-functional collaboration with product managers, designers, and other engineers to define software
  requirements, devise solution strategies, and ensure seamless integration and alignment with business objectives."
* "Collaboration with peers, participation in code reviews, and promotion of a culture of code quality and
  knowledge sharing."
* "Adherence to secure coding practices to mitigate vulnerabilities, protect sensitive data, and ensure secure
  software solutions."
* "Implementation of effective unit testing practices to ensure proper code design, readability, and reliability."

**Analyst-grade expectations** [2]: meet stakeholder and customer needs through operational excellence; perform
prescribed activities to a high standard; no people leadership at this grade; identify and escalate policy
breaches; take ownership for managing risk and strengthening controls; work within well-defined procedures;
"make judgements based on the analysis of factual information"; build stakeholder relationships.

**Entry requirements** [2]:

* **3 A-levels at BBB (or equivalent)**
* **GCSE Mathematics and English Language at grade A*–C / 9–4 (or equivalent)**
* Must meet the Government's apprentice funding requirements, including a **3-year residence requirement** (subject
  to exceptions)
* Legal right to work for the full duration (August 2027 – August 2031) at the point of enrolment, with no gaps and
  no dependency on future visa applications
* Must not have prior study, qualifications, apprenticeships, prior-work-experience competence, or prior funding
  that makes you ineligible for Government apprentice funding
* Note: if you already have a degree, Barclays directs you to its graduate route instead [2]

**One-programme rule:** Barclays states applicants "can only apply to one programme per six-month period" [3]. This
matters — applying to Technology Developer Northampton rules out Technology Developer Knutsford and Technology
Analyst for six months.

## 5. Application Process Steps

Barclays publishes the stages on the listing itself [2]:

1. **Application review** — "We'll review your application"
2. **Online assessments** — "You'll be asked to complete our online assessments"
3. **Eligibility survey** — "We'll ask you to complete a survey, so we can be sure of your eligibility"
4. **In-person assessment day** — "you'll be assessed during a group activity and interview (this will take place
   from **October 2026**)"
5. **Outcome and feedback** — "We'll let you know the outcome and give you feedback"

Barclays' general apprentice application page adds detail [3]:

* **Application form:** covers work experience and academic background, takes roughly **30 minutes**.
* **Initial assessment:** **two interactive assessments** measuring workplace performance and preferred working
  styles. Can be done on any device. Barclays provides "full instructions about the structure and timings" and
  access to **practice tests**. You get **60 minutes** to complete them, within **five calendar days** of invitation.
* **Interview stage:** a **motivational interview plus group activity** with members of the leadership team. A
  chance to "discuss strengths, motivations, and ask programme questions".
* **No AI tools in the interview** — including transcription bots and meeting assistants. Breach means the
  application is withdrawn [3].
* **If unsuccessful:** you get a personalised feedback report and can reapply after six months [3].

**Timeline reality check:** listing live 9 Sept → closes 25 Sept → assessment days from October 2026 → programme
starts August 2027 [2].

## 6. Application Tailoring Tips

**Apply this week, not next.** The listing says explicitly that it "may close ahead of the advertised date due to
application volumes" [2]. The form is only about 30 minutes [3]. There is no upside to waiting.

**You have a Barclays connection already — use it.** Your CV records a week at Barclays Corporate Bank, Canary
Wharf, in July 2025, including a team case study analysing financial documents and presenting a lending decision to
a panel. That is directly relevant: it is Barclays-specific, it evidences the "commitment and enthusiasm" Barclays
asks for [2], and the case study maps onto "make judgements based on the analysis of factual information" [2]. This
is the single strongest differentiator you have for this application.

**Northampton is a genuine advantage, so say so.** The role is at Pavilion Drive, Northampton [2], and you are at
school in Northampton. Barclays sets a 3-year residence requirement [2] and the site is a physical campus [2].
Local commitment is worth a sentence.

**Mirror the two frameworks by name.** Barclays states apprentices must demonstrate the Values (Respect, Integrity,
Service, Excellence, Stewardship) and the Mindset (Empower, Challenge, Drive) [2]. Use those exact words. "Service"
in Barclays' definition is "empathy and humility, putting the people and businesses we serve at the centre" [1] —
your cricket club volunteering, teaching children, is a Service and Respect story.

**Lead on security and reliability, not just building things.** Two of the five stated accountabilities are about
secure coding and unit testing [2], and Barclays' own research output is about cyber and AI investment [7]. A
banking developer is judged on not breaking things. Any project where you thought about what could go wrong beats a
project that merely worked.

**Emphasise collaboration and code review.** Two more accountabilities are explicitly about cross-functional
collaboration and peer code review [2]. Your Barclays case study was a team of four; your work experience at Toru
Digital covered Git [own CV]. Git and code review connect directly.

**Prepare for the group activity specifically.** The assessment day is a group activity plus interview [2][3]. This
is where Respect ("value everyone's contribution") [1] and the Empower mindset [2] get observed. Drawing quieter
people in scores better than leading loudly.

**Do the practice tests.** Barclays provides them [3]. The window is 60 minutes inside five days [3], so there is no
room to be surprised by the format.

**Decide your one application carefully.** One programme per six months [3]. Technology Developer Northampton is the
right pick over Knutsford on location, and over Technology Analyst if you want to write code rather than do business
analysis [4].

**Likely interview questions (unverified opinion, inferred from the Values, Mindset and stated process):**

* Why Barclays, and why an apprenticeship over university? *(motivational interview [3])*
* Tell us about a time you set a higher standard than was asked of you. *(Excellence [1])*
* Tell us about a time you disagreed with a group. *(Challenge [2], Integrity [1])*
* Describe a time you left something better than you found it. *(Stewardship [1])*
* Tell us about a time you helped someone else succeed. *(Respect, Empower [1][2])*
* What interests you about technology in banking specifically? *(AI and cyber are live themes [6][7][8])*
